## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a bespoke, high-touch digital experience for discerning automotive enthusiasts and VIP clients seeking exotic and luxury vehicle rentals. It balances the quiet refinement of high-end hospitality with the aerodynamic precision and tactile luxury of modern hypercars. 

The aesthetic is anchored in a warm, architectural minimalism layered with tactile depth. Rather than cold, clinical tech-monochrome, the interface employs an organic warm cream foundation accented by rich obsidian slate and luminous amber-gold accents. Surfaces feel sculpted and substantial: floating pill silhouettes, deeply diffused warm shadows, high-clarity micro-details, and generous breathing room evoke the prestige of an exclusive private concourse.

## Colors
The palette relies on deliberate contrast between light architectural backdrops and dark automotive structural blocks, ignited by precise golden touches:

- **Primary Accent (`#F5A623` / `#E69516`):** Amber gold, reserved for critical conversion touchpoints, high-value highlights, active interactive states, and luxury accreditation markers. Never overused; applied like metallic trim on an engineered dashboard.
- **Secondary / Charcoal Base (`#1E2124` / `#16181D`):** Deep volcanic slate used for prominent floating navigational panels, luxury dark mode modules, heavy action buttons, and dominant headings.
- **Backdrop & Canvas (`#F3EFEA`):** Warm gallery cream that softens visual fatigue and sets an editorial, high-fashion tone distinct from standard sterile white interfaces.
- **Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure crystalline white surfaces that float crisp and unblemished above the warm beige ground.
- **Muted & Neutral Text (`#7E8289`):** Neutral warm slate engineered for spec labels, secondary telemetry, and metadata without reducing legibility.
- **Dark Body & Titles (`#1E2124`):** Solid charcoal for primary text, avoiding harsh synthetic `#000000`.

## Typography
The system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across display, body, and micro-labels to convey sculpted geometric modernity. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in bold and extra-bold weights with tight negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic the stance and aerodynamic contours of luxury coachwork.
- **Editorial Sub-Headings:** Often juxtapose charcoal text with single-word amber accents or small uppercase super-headers.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Horsepower, acceleration, and rental day rates benefit from the font’s geometric tabular cleanliness, ensuring telemetry data lines up smoothly.
- **Micro-Labels & Status Indicators:** Use uppercase styling at 11px–12px with widened tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.08em`) to guarantee quick recognition on mobile viewports.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout leverages a fluid 12-column responsive architecture with wide external canvas boundaries, reinforcing an airy, showroom-like gallery feel.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with a fixed max-width container of 1440px, 24px gutters, and 48px outer margins. Floating interactive elements (such as side navigation rails and booking filter cards) break standard content tracks by using layered off-grid z-indices.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with 20px gutters and 32px outer canvas margins. Vehicle spec grids condense to 2-column or 3-column rows. Floating sidebars retract into bottom sheets or collapsible header trays.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px gutters and 20px side margins. Filter chips become horizontally scrolling rails, and hero showcase media switches to edge-to-edge aspect ratios with overlay pill navigation.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Component interiors follow an 8pt modular interval (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`). Section separators maintain large gaps (` space-xl` or dynamic `4rem–6rem`) to let high-resolution automotive imagery breathe.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is modeled through gentle, warm ambient light, treating UI elements as sculpted physical surfaces resting over a suede-like canvas:

- **Level 1 (Subtle Inset / Spec Wells):** Inner shadows or faint neutral tone-shifts (`#EBE6DF`) applied to secondary badge containers and inactive input fields.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Showcase Units):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces lifted with a warm-tinted diffused shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(30, 33, 36, 0.06), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(30, 33, 36, 0.02)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Sidebars & Docked Nav):** Elevated dark slate (`#1E2124`) or pure white panels utilizing an ambient suspended shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(22, 24, 29, 0.18), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(22, 24, 29, 0.08)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Checkout Sheets):** `0 36px 72px -16px rgba(22, 24, 29, 0.28)`, backed by an ultra-soft frosted scrim (`rgba(243, 239, 234, 0.72)` with a 16px blur) keeping context visible yet tranquil.

## Shapes
The shape language is aerodynamic and welcoming, utilizing generous pill radiuses that soften structural density:

- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`):** Standard for all interactive buttons, filter chips, search query pills, and reservation step trackers.
- **Extra Large Curvature (`rounded-3xl` / 1.5rem to 2rem):** Used for primary vehicle display cards, floating side navigation drawers, and segmented visual containers.
- **Medium Rounding (`rounded-xl` / 0.75rem):** Reserved strictly for nested elements within cards (e.g., individual technical spec chips, telemetry mini-charts, and input borders).

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Amber Gold):** Background `#F5A623`, text `#1E2124` (Bold), fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `14px 28px`. Hover shifts to `#E69516` with a soft gold glow (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Luxury Action (Charcoal):** Background `#1E2124`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#16181D`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Action:** Transparent background, 1.5px border `#EBE6DF`, text `#1E2124`. Hover transitions to solid `#FFFFFF` with default Level 1 elevation.

### 2. Vehicle Showcase Cards
- **Architecture:** Pure white card surface with `rounded-3xl` corners, framed by an ultra-soft border (`rgba(30, 33, 36, 0.04)`).
- **Visual Zone:** Top section features dynamic transparent 3/4 car render hovering slightly over the boundary.
- **Card Header & Pricing:** Model name in `headline-sm` with brand marquee in `label-sm` (uppercase `#7E8289`). Rate displayed as bold hero numbers (`$1,250`) paired with a muted `/day` indicator.
- **Action Footprint:** Includes a direct "Reserve" pill button along with an integrated quick-save heart toggle.

### 3. Spec Badges & Data Chips
- **Container:** Compact horizontal capsules (`rounded-full` or `rounded-xl`), filled with background `#F3EFEA` or white.
- **Iconography & Metric:** Crisp 16px monochrome vector icon (engine, top speed speedometer, 0-60 timer, transmission) paired with high-contrast text (`15px` 600 weight) and label below in `#7E8289`.

### 4. Interactive Badge Filters
- **Default State:** Pill button (`rounded-full`) in white `#FFFFFF` with muted slate text `#7E8289`, subtle border `#E5E0D8`.
- **Active State:** Deep charcoal `#1E2124` fill with pure white typography, or amber `#F5A623` with dark text when highlighting special categories (e.g., "Hypercars", "Chauffeur Included").

### 5. Status Tags
- **Approved / Ready:** Soft sage background (`rgba(52, 168, 83, 0.12)`), text `#1E7E34`, pill radius, uppercase `label-sm` with a solid dot prefix.
- **Pending:** Amber gold tint (`rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.14)`), text `#B87405`.
- **Under Review:** Cool charcoal tone (`rgba(30, 33, 36, 0.08)`), text `#1E2124`.

### 6. Floating Navigation & Sidebars
- **Desktop Concierge Dock:** Suspended vertical or horizontal navigation dock with `rounded-full` or `rounded-3xl` styling, filled with dark charcoal `#1E2124`.
- **Interactive Details:** Icon-only states with high-contrast active states illuminated by `#F5A623` ambient indicator dots.

### 7. Form Inputs & Date Range Selectors
- **Fields:** Warm white `#FFFFFF` fill with `rounded-full` or `rounded-2xl` corners, inset 16px horizontal padding, placeholder text in `#7E8289`. Focus triggers an amber border glow (`0 0 0 2px #F5A623`).
